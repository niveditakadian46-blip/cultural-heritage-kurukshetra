Useful Information

Before You Visit

Kurukshetra is easiest to explore in comfortable clothing and footwear. Carry drinking water, especially during warm weather, and keep some time for travelling between different sites.

Respectful Visits

Many places in Kurukshetra are religious and culturally important. Visitors should dress respectfully, remove footwear where required, and follow signs or instructions at temples and sacred areas.

Getting Around

Local transport, taxis and auto-rickshaws can help visitors travel between the railway station, bus stand, Brahma Sarovar, Jyotisar and other important places.

Plan Your Journey

1. Choose Your Places

Choose the places you would like to visit, such as Brahma Sarovar, Jyotisar, Sthaneshwar Mahadev Temple, Kurukshetra Panorama and Science Centre, or museums and heritage sites.

2. Plan Your Route

Group nearby places together to save travelling time. Start early if you want to visit several locations in one day, especially during weekends or religious festivals.

3. Keep Essentials Ready

Carry water, a phone charger, comfortable footwear and a small bag. Check local opening times before visiting museums or public attractions.

4. Ask For Help If Needed

Use the Contact Us page for general support and local guidance while planning your visit.

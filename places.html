Important Places in Kurukshetra

Explore sacred sites, peaceful sarovars and inspiring museums.

Places to Explore

Kurukshetra has sacred places, peaceful sarovars, temples and museums. Know about the categories below to discover places that interest you.

Temples in Kurukshetra

Explore important temples connected with faith, pilgrimage and the Mahabharata tradition.

Jyotisar

Jyotisar is a sacred place traditionally connected with the Bhagavad Gita. According to Hindu tradition, Lord Krishna gave Arjuna guidance here before the Mahabharata battle. It is a peaceful place for the visitors who want to learn about the spiritual importance of Kurukshetra.

Sthaneshwar Mahadev Temple

Sthaneshwar Mahadev Temple is an important Shiva temple in Thanesar, Kurukshetra. It is visited by devotees throughout the year, especially during Mahashivratri. According to local religious tradition, Lord Krishna and the Pandavas visited this place before the Mahabharata battle.

Bhadrakali Temple

Bhadrakali Temple, also known as Shri Devi Koop Temple, is an important Shakti temple in Kurukshetra. It is visited by devotees seeking blessings and taking part in worship. According to local tradition, the Pandavas came here to seek the goddess’s blessings before the Mahabharata battle.

Mata Bala Sundari Temple

Mata Bala Sundari Temple is a respected place of worship in Kurukshetra, visited by devotees seeking the blessings of the goddess. The temple is especially lively during religious festivals, when visitors gather for prayers and traditional celebrations.

Venkateshwar Balaji Temple

Venkateshwar Balaji Temple is a Hindu temple dedicated to Lord Venkateshwar, a form of Lord Vishnu. It gives visitors another peaceful place for prayer and worship in Kurukshetra. The temple reflects the religious diversity of the city and is visited by devotees and tourists throughout the year.

Museums and Learning Places

Discover museums and learning places that share the history, archaeology and cultural traditions of Kurukshetra.

Kurukshetra Panorama and Science Centre

Kurukshetra Panorama and Science Centre presents the story of the Mahabharata through a large circular panorama, displays and educational exhibits. It is a useful place for visitors and students to learn about the cultural importance of Kurukshetra in an engaging way.

Krishna Museum

Krishna Museum displays sculptures, paintings and other objects connected with Lord Krishna and the Mahabharata tradition. Its collections help visitors understand the religious art and cultural stories that are important to Kurukshetra.

Mahabharata Anubhav Kendra, Jyotisar

Mahabharata Anubhav Kendra is an interactive museum and experience centre at Jyotisar. It uses modern displays to share stories from the Mahabharata in an engaging way. Visitors can learn about the epic, the Bhagavad Gita and the cultural importance of Jyotisar through immersive exhibitions.

Dharohar Haryana Cultural Museum

Dharohar Haryana Cultural Museum is located at Kurukshetra University. It presents the traditions, crafts, clothing and daily life of Haryana through cultural displays. It helps visitors understand the local heritage of Haryana and the cultural setting of Kurukshetra.

Kalpana Chawla Memorial Planetarium

Kalpana Chawla Memorial Planetarium is near Jyotisar on Pehowa Road. It was created to help visitors, especially students, learn about astronomy, space and the universe. The planetarium includes a dome theatre, astronomy gallery and Astro Park, making it an interesting learning destination in Kurukshetra.

Sarovars and Sacred Water Bodies

Explore peaceful sarovars and sacred water bodies that are important for pilgrimage, worship and the cultural traditions of Kurukshetra.

Brahma Sarovar

Brahma Sarovar is one of the best-known sacred water bodies in Kurukshetra. It is a major place for pilgrims who come to take part in religious rituals and prayers. The sarovar is especially well known during the International Gita Mahotsav, when its surroundings are decorated with many lamps and cultural events.

Sannihit Sarovar

Sannihit Sarovar is another important sacred water body in Kurukshetra. Pilgrims visit it for prayer, ritual bathing and religious ceremonies. It is particularly significant during solar eclipses and important Hindu festivals, when many devotees come to Kurukshetra.

Bhishma Kund

Bhishma Kund is a sacred site in Narkatari, Kurukshetra. It is traditionally connected with Bhishma Pitamah from the Mahabharata. According to tradition, Arjuna created a water source here to give water to Bhishma while he lay on the bed of arrows. The site is visited by people interested in the Mahabharata and local heritage.

Jyotisar Sarovar

Jyotisar Sarovar is a peaceful sacred water body near the Jyotisar pilgrimage site. It is visited by people who come to learn about the Bhagavad Gita and the Mahabharata tradition. The surrounding area is important for spiritual tourism and is especially visited during Gita-related celebrations.

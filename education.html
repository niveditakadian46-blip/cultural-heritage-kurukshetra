💡 Did You Know?

  • Kurukshetra is strongly connected with the Mahabharata and Bhagavad Gita traditions.
  • Jyotisar is traditionally associated with Lord Krishna’s guidance to Arjuna.
  • Kurukshetra has important temples, sacred sarovars, museums and learning centres.
  • The International Gita Mahotsav celebrates the cultural and spiritual importance of the region.
  • Kurukshetra has a long archaeological history, with some local pottery evidence linked to pre-Harappan and Harappan periods.
  • Brahma Sarovar is one of Kurukshetra’s best-known sacred water bodies and is an important place during the International Gita Mahotsav.
  • Kurukshetra University was founded in 1956 and is one of Haryana’s major higher-education institutions.
  • National Institute of Technology Kurukshetra began in 1963 as a Regional Engineering College before becoming NIT Kurukshetra.
  • Kalpana Chawla Memorial Planetarium near Jyotisar includes a dome theatre, astronomy gallery and Astro Park for science learning.
  • Mahabharata Anubhav Kendra at Jyotisar uses interactive displays to help visitors explore stories connected with the Mahabharata.
